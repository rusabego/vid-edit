# Edit rules

Look at the reference frames before you choose a shot. Match the rhythm you can see: how long a shot holds, whether the join is a cut, a white flash, a fade through black, or a panel collage.

A white flash is a short `fadewhite`, at or under 0.3 seconds, then the next shot. Do not dissolve two different angles of the same subject. Use a cut or `fadeblack` for that join.

A panel collage is the `collage` segment (`2v`, `2h`, or `4`) with a gap. It is the available cousin of a rounded multi-panel preset, not a copy of that preset's mask.

For a Reel, keep type below about 13% of the height and above about 79% of the height. The bottom band is where the caption and buttons sit.

The score is original, or it is a file the user has the rights to. Never take audio from the reference. `boutique` is a dry beat with the drums left unducked. `cinematic` is a pad that lifts once. Say which one you used.

Use only words the user, or the footage itself, actually gives you. Do not invent a URL, a handle, or a slogan.

Phone and chat footage stays soft when the frame is 1080 wide. Graphic cards and lookbook pages are sharp. Say that if someone expects a 4K picture from a small source.

Python packages and FFmpeg belong to the project `.venv`. Do not install them into the system Python.

After the file is written, look at stills from the start, the middle of each segment, and the last second.
